# Restyler Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 22 Sept, 2026**

Restyler is a Chrome extension that lets you change how websites look.

## What Restyler stores

Restyler saves the following **only on your device**, using Chrome's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`):

- The hostnames of websites you restyle, and when you last changed them
- Your style changes for each site: the CSS selector and a short text label for each element you pick, the colors, sizes and visibility you chose, quick-style settings (dark mode, hide images, text size), any custom CSS you write, and whether Restyler is on or off for that site
- Your theme choice for the Restyler popup (Bright, Dark or Auto)

## What Restyler does not do

- It does not send any data to the developer or any third party. Restyler makes no network requests.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies.
- It does not sell, share or transfer your data.
- It does not require an account or sign-in.

## Site icons

The "All sites" list shows site icons from Chrome's own local favicon cache. No request is made to the websites or to any outside service to fetch them.

## Deleting your data

You can remove any site's changes from the extension's "All sites" tab. Uninstalling Restyler deletes all of its stored data.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
